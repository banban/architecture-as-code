## Recommendations

State required catalogue updates, architecture decisions, roadmap changes, and communication or approval actions.

## Example

New privacy classification evidence changes `REQ-004` from Should to Must. The impact is a revised data-handling control, an updated `DATA-002` classification, a new ADR, and an additional security gate in `WP-003`.

## TOGAF 10 alignment

This supports Requirements Management and Change Management. It ensures that new facts, risks, or stakeholder concerns are assessed systematically rather than changing the architecture or backlog without traceability.
